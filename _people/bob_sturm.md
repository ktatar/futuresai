## Bio

Sturm is an associate professor in computer science. From 2020-2025 he led the ERC project "MUSAiC: Music at the Frontiers of Artificial Creativity and Criticism" [(https://musaiclab.wordpress.com)](https://musaiclab.wordpress.com). His research is in computer music, music informatics, and music and machine learning. He is a composer (e.g., [https://youtu.be/TwWsEJox2aY](https://youtu.be/TwWsEJox2aY)) and plays Irish traditional accordion and machine folk ([https://tunesfromtheaifrontiers.wordpress.com](https://tunesfromtheaifrontiers.wordpress.com)).
