## Bio

Fredrik Tell is Professor and Chair at the Department of of Business Studies and affiliated professor at Uppsala Centre for Business History, Uppsala University. He has broad research experience in innovation studies, strategic management and business history, covering areas such as e.g. strategic temporal work, technology diffusion, markets for technology, industrial dynamics, organizational capabilities, project-based organizations and global value chains. He is leading the Uppsala Patent History Group (<https://www.uphg.se>), the founders of the Swedish Historical Patent Infrastructure (<https://svenskahistoriskapatent.se/EN/>).
