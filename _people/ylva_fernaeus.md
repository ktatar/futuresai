## Bio

I am Professor in Design at Umeå University and also works at KTH Royal Institute of Technology. My work concerns the design of interactions as they take place with physical things, blending critical theory with creative practice, at the intersections of technology, culture, and design. As co-director of UmArts, I lead projects on Art and AI and Postindustrial Making, investigating how tech-driven design practices shape contemporary knowledge-making.
