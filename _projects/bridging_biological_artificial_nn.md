# Description

Supported by the WASP Starting Package, this initiative aims to bridge the gap between artificial intelligence and neuroscience. By analysing learning mechanisms in both synthetic and biological systems, we aim to derive insights that can lead to more robust AI models and a deeper understanding of neural computation.
